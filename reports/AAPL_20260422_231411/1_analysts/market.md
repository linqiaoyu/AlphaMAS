# AAPL Technical Analysis Report - March 5, 2024

## Executive Summary

Apple Inc. (AAPL) is currently experiencing a **significant technical breakdown** with multiple bearish signals across all timeframes. The stock has plunged below critical support levels, entering deeply oversold territory with accelerating downward momentum. The current price of $173.52 (as of March 4, 2024) represents a dramatic decline from recent highs, signaling potential capitulation selling.

## Detailed Technical Analysis

### **Trend Analysis: Bearish Across All Timeframes**

**Long-Term Trend (200 SMA):** AAPL closed at $173.52 on March 4, which is **$8.34 below the 200-day SMA** ($181.86). This represents a **4.6% discount** to the long-term trend benchmark. The 200 SMA has been trending slightly upward but the price has decisively broken below this critical level, indicating a potential long-term trend reversal.

**Medium-Term Trend (50 SMA):** The situation is even more concerning on the 50-day SMA front. The current price is **$11.59 below the 50 SMA** ($185.11), representing a **6.3% discount**. More importantly, the 50 SMA has been **declining consistently** from $188.63 on February 5 to $184.62 on March 5, showing clear medium-term bearish momentum.

**Short-Term Trend (10 EMA):** The 10 EMA has collapsed from $186.78 on February 9 to $177.25 on March 5. The current price is **$3.73 below the 10 EMA**, confirming the short-term downtrend. The 10 EMA has been declining at an accelerating rate, indicating worsening short-term momentum.

### **Momentum Indicators: Extreme Bearish Signals**

**MACD Analysis:** The MACD line has turned deeply negative, reaching -3.15 on March 5 from -0.23 on February 9. The **MACD signal line** is at -2.09, and the MACD line remains below the signal line, confirming the bearish momentum. The **histogram (MACD - Signal)** is -1.06, showing expanding negative momentum.

**Critical Observation:** The MACD has been negative since early February and has **accelerated its decline** in recent days, suggesting increasing selling pressure rather than stabilization.

**RSI Analysis - Extreme Oversold Conditions:** The RSI reading of **23.27 on March 5** represents **severely oversold conditions**. This is below the traditional oversold threshold of 30 and approaching extreme levels. The RSI has declined from 50.22 on February 9, showing consistent deterioration in momentum.

**Important Context:** While extreme RSI readings often precede bounces, in strong downtrends, RSI can remain oversold for extended periods. The rapid decline from 36.18 on March 1 to 23.27 on March 5 suggests **panic selling** rather than orderly distribution.

### **Volatility and Price Boundaries**

**Bollinger Bands Analysis:** The current price of $173.52 is **well below the Bollinger Lower Band** of $172.06, indicating the stock is trading outside its normal volatility range. This represents a **breakdown below statistical support**.

**Band Width Dynamics:** The upper band has declined from $194.56 to $190.56 while the lower band has dropped from $179.87 to $172.06. The **band contraction followed by expansion** suggests volatility was compressing before the recent breakdown.

**Critical Support/Resistance Levels:**

- **Next Resistance:** 10 EMA at $177.25

**ATR Analysis:** The Average True Range has increased from 3.21 on February 9 to **3.40 on March 5**, indicating **rising volatility**. This 6% increase in ATR confirms that the recent price movements represent genuine volatility expansion rather than noise.

**Risk Management Implications:** With an ATR of 3.40, daily moves of approximately ±$3.40 should be considered normal volatility. The recent decline from $178.04 to $173.52 represents a move of -$4.52, which exceeds one ATR, confirming the significance of the breakdown.
