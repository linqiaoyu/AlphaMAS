With revenue declining and its key profit center under attack, the multiple is a vulnerability, not a testament to quality. The bull's hope rests on a spectacular WWDC AI reveal that immediately changes the narrative. That's a binary bet, not a foundation for investment. The bear's case is built on observable, present-tense breakdowns in price, policy, and geopolitics.
